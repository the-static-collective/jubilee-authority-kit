# Ecosystem adoption map

## Layering

```
Project0 / TranchNode
  canonical addressing, bridge rules, constitutional grammar
          ↓
Jubilee Authority Kit
  command admission, receipts, refusal, replay, projection covenant
          ↓
domain runtimes and adapters
  Gramfork · Groove Rooms · Formation Trace · Grove surfaces
```

This is a shared protocol boundary, not an instruction to merge repositories or flatten their domains.

## Placement

### Gramfork — first proving ground

Gramfork is the first serious non-music domain node. Keep its mutual-aid ontology local: circles, needs, provisioning, verification, and human relationships. Reuse the kit’s command envelope, expected-head behavior, idempotency, immutable receipts, and replay fixtures. A “provisioned” display must be a projection of an admitted receipt, not a browser-side status change.

### Groove Rooms — listening-room adapter

Groove Rooms consumes the live-event-field ideas of Band Runtime. Keep rooms, invite tokens, original audio bytes, waveform decoding, voluntary follow-playback, and media affordances in its adapter. Use the kit to make invited actions, contribution anchoring, protected silence, refusal proof, and branch restoration testable. Mix/playback state remains view-level authority only.

### Formation Trace — local instrument

Formation Trace is intentionally more private than a ledger. Raw editing events, deletion, annotation, and evaluation may remain mutable/local according to the author’s privacy policy. The bridge is not “sync the database”; it is a separately offered, signed export artifact with declared disclosure and receivable-as conditions.

### Jubilee Workspace and Idea Grove — projection/proposal surfaces

These are expressive interfaces for nearby work. They may read projections, show lineage, gather an offered proposal, and explain an admission outcome. They should not write canonical tables directly. Their present UI grammar is useful; their authority must pass through the kit.

### FORK! — independent consumer experiment

FORK! remains a standalone creative product. Its candidate roles and parent-linked records are valuable interaction research. It may export a proposal into a grove or other domain boundary, but should not be made to carry the entire Jubilee ontology.

### Corpus OS local intake

Keep this either as a local adapter for `corpus-os` or a clearly parked experiment. Its meaningful seam is intake: how local material becomes an offered artifact with declared provenance and disclosure. It should not become a parallel authority center.

## Non-negotiable integration rule

No domain surface gets a special shortcut around admission. If a UI needs a direct table write to “work,” it is describing an adapter gap, not permission to bypass the boundary.
